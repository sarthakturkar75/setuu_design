Lesson Detail

Concrete Curing Delays in Sub-Zero Temps

Quality Foundation

Description of Observation

During the pouring of section 4B foundation walls, unexpected temperature drops (reaching -2°C overnight) resulted in delayed curing times. Initial slump tests were nominal, but 7-day strength tests showed a 15% reduction in expected PSI.

Root Cause Analysis

Weather forecasting failure. Site manager relied on a 48-hour generic forecast rather than the localized micro-climate engineering weather service. Thermal blanketing was not staged on-site prior to pour.

Mitigation / Recommendation

1. Implement mandatory check of localized specialized weather service (e.g., ClearPath) 12 hours prior to any major pour during shoulder seasons.

2. Thermal blankets must be physically staged on the pouring deck, not in the central laydown yard, before the first truck arrives if overnight temps are forecast below 5°C.
