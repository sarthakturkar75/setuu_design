ID: LL-2023-004

Steel Grade Mismatch in Phase 2 Structural Support

Root Cause

Supplier substituted Grade 50 steel for Grade 60 without triggering the automated structural variance alert in the procurement API.

Recommendation

Mandate physical tensile strength validation scans prior to staging, logging results directly against the PO hash.

ID: LL-2023-089

Logistics Delay: Heavy Machinery Cross-Border Customs Hold

Root Cause

Missing sub-component compliance certificates (Form 3A) for hydraulic assemblies caused a 14-day quarantine at port.

Recommendation

Integrate automated vendor portal checklist for all international freight, requiring PDF upload of Form 3A before dispatch authorization.
